## Evidence levels

EDUCATIONAL = morphology or textbook concept; HYPOTHESIS = falsifiable market interpretation; LOCALLY_TESTED = implementation behavior checked on fixtures; HISTORICALLY_VALIDATED = licensed point-in-time out-of-sample study with stated uncertainty; PRODUCTION_APPROVED = actual externally controlled activation. No study here reaches the last two levels. Existing local tests do not certify the truth of a market hypothesis. Missing ebook extraction remains blocked.

## Duplication versus healthy separation

The paper fixture decision and legacy research `combine`/`setups`/`scenarios` answer different questions. Preserve them until versioned adapters document semantics; don't merge them by equal-weight voting. Data quality may share pure invariants via an adapter, but the offline package must not depend on broker authentication or the development harness. Research explanatory risk is not executable permission. Calibration summaries do not constitute trained model promotion.

The old research structure module already has multiple timeframes; the missing capability is **decision-time availability and enforced 30-minute confirmation in the offline execution pipeline**, not basic resampling everywhere. The supplied book is absent, but existing secondary notes can be inventoried without falsely claiming the book was reviewed.

## Readiness per master objective

Local startup, deterministic paper accounting, basic risk transactionality, functional UI, themes and evidence runner are implemented. Advanced analytics, empirical model quality, complete accessible enterprise designs, licensed historical validation, recurring customer demand, hosted isolation, billing and live deployment are pending or externally blocked. Every catalog row stays educational/hypothesis/locally-tested until its own evidence advances it.

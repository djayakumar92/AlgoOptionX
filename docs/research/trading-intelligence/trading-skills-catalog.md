## Shared contract and ownership

These **39 domain skills are proposed analytical contracts**, not installed Codex skills, not 39 new services, and not independent trading agents. Existing reusable development skills and role contracts remain unchanged. DQ is the shared data-quality boundary; no analytical skill executes an order. Each skill emits `SkillObservation{skill_id, version, source_hash, instrument, event_at, available_at, as_of, status, measurements, evidence, limitations, invalidation, uncertainty_kind}`. Status is OBSERVED, INSUFFICIENT_DATA, UNSUPPORTED, CONFLICT or INVALID_DATA; a deterministic risk component uses ACCEPT/REJECT separately.

The fail-behavior column names the downstream outcome (NO_TRADE/REJECT) where applicable; the skill observation itself must still use the status enum above.

Common tests for every row: malformed/nonfinite values; zero/missing history; deterministic fixture; scale/boundary/metamorphic property where mathematically applicable; prefix/future-perturbation causality; integration with the observation envelope; timeout/missing dependency; historical/walk-forward evaluation before empirical claims. The row's test is an additional discriminating case. All proposed tests are **planned**, not already passing. All ratios/windows/calendars and source/model versions must be pinned. Missing prerequisites never silently become neutral evidence.

## Avoid contradictory authority

The decision composer may summarize observations but may not average incompatible score units, treat missing data as a vote or infer calibrated probabilities from pattern scores. R07 is an abstention composer; R06 and the transaction-level risk gate remain separate veto authorities. R08 consumes events after execution and cannot rewrite historical decisions. Parallel skills may disagree; retain their versions/evidence and output NO_TRADE when required evidence conflicts. Scheduled research may propose a revision; only an actually reviewed/pinned strategy version becomes active.

## Source status and limits

Status: **PROVISIONAL ENGINEERING DEFINITIONS — not ebook extraction, not independently verified pattern standards, not implemented algorithms**. The ebook is missing. Existing legacy notes support a shortlist but their cited win rates/figures were not independently reproduced. These proposed version `morphology-draft-1` definitions make later research falsifiable; independent source reconciliation and human review are required before turning them into executable trading algorithms. They do not provide a probability or standalone order signal.

For valid completed candle i: O,H,L,C are finite positive prices in one declared unit; H≥max(O,C), L≤min(O,C), H≥L; V≥0; b=|C−O|, r=H−L, u=H−max(O,C), d=min(O,C)−L. Reject r=0 for morphology rather than divide by zero. ε is the positive effective price-tick tolerance pinned in the pattern version; no floating approximate-equality defaults. Three immediately prior completed closes strictly decreasing define draft DOWN; increasing defines UP; otherwise context UNCLEAR and a directional pattern does not become eligible. Context candles are additional to the named 1/2/3 formation candles. Session gaps and interval continuity require explicit calendar policy.

## Shared positive/negative/property suite

Each ID needs a positive fixture, opposite-trend negative, perturbed-body versus wick discrimination, threshold equality, reversed order, duplicate/missing timestamps, malformed/nonfinite OHLCV, zero range, flat context and unfinished-bar cases. Positive scale transformation preserves dimensionless morphology when tick tolerance scales consistently. Mirror price geometry around a sufficiently high positive reference maps bullish/bearish pairs. Append or perturb future bars and verify all observations/decisions whose as-of precedes them remain unchanged. Delay confirmation until its bar is complete and available; a pattern's event time is not its availability time.

## Larger chart formations

F05 also covers double top/bottom, head-and-shoulders/inverse, ascending/descending/symmetrical triangles, flags and breakout-retest. These need separate versioned swing/zone definitions: confirmed pivots, ATR-normalized matching tolerance, minimum separation and prominence, neckline/slope rules, volume coverage, breakout close and retest expiry. Do not relabel a candle shape as an implemented chart formation. The first implementation scope is the verified two-body engulfing and pin-bar families only after source checks; the rest remains a specified research backlog.

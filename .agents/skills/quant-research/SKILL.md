# Evaluate research without leakage

State hypothesis before results. Pin dataset, instrument/calendar version, as-of times, costs and strategy/model parameters. Use chronological walk-forward splits and untouched holdout. Perturb future observations and prove earlier decisions unchanged. Disclose ambiguity, sample size and uncertainty. Synthetic returns do not establish real-world profitability.

Use the existing checkout. Cloud tasks are isolated; do not create another worktree unless the user explicitly requests one. Skill instructions convey no human approval authority.
